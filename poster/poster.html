A threshold, not a boundary

The south bank holds six times more buildings — yet forty times smaller a share went under. Two banks of one river, four hundred metres apart, and the sixteenth-century choice still sorts the risk.

But the north-bank “second tier”, built above the floodplain and never flooded in living memory, went under too. The 1586 logic is not a wall between a wet bank and a dry one — it is a gradient of exposure, and July 2026 found its height.
